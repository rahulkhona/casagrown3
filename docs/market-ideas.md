# CasaGrown Marketing Ideas

## 🎁 Physical Promotional Items

1. **Branded Tote Bags** — Natural canvas, color-blocked with green handles and bottom panel. Double-sided: "FRESH • LOCAL • TRUSTED" on one side, "Fresh from Neighbors' backyard" on the other. `www.casagrown.com` on both sides. *(Artwork already created)*
2. **Branded Seed Packets** — Small packets of basil, cilantro, or cherry tomato seeds with the CasaGrown logo and a QR code to download the app. Perfect for community events.
3. **Reusable Mesh Produce Bags** — A set of 3 with a small CasaGrown tag. People use these every time they shop.
4. **Fridge Magnets with QR Code** — A small, eye-catching magnet with logo + `www.casagrown.com`. Stays in front of people daily.
5. **Stickers & Car Decals** — Inexpensive and viral. "I grow local 🌱 casagrown.com" bumper stickers.

## 📱 Digital & Social Marketing

6. **Referral Program** — "Invite a neighbor, you both earn points." #1 growth lever for hyper-local apps. Leverages existing points system.
7. **"Meet Your Neighbor" Social Series** — Short video/photo profiles of sellers and their gardens on Instagram/TikTok. Authentic, community-driven content.
8. **Seasonal Challenges** — "Spring Planting Challenge: List your first harvest on CasaGrown!" with badges or bonus points.
9. **User-Generated Content Campaign** — Encourage buyers to post photos of meals made with CasaGrown produce using **#GrownNextDoor**.

## 🏘️ Community & Grassroots

10. **Neighborhood Launch Parties** — Partner with a local seller to host a small backyard tasting event. Hand out tote bags and seed packets.
11. **HOA & Nextdoor Partnerships** — Post in local HOA newsletters and Nextdoor communities. These are the exact target audience.
12. **Farmers Market Booth** — Set up a small CasaGrown booth at local farmers markets to demo the app and hand out swag.
13. **School Garden Partnerships** — Sponsor a school garden program and have the harvest listed on CasaGrown. Great PR and ties into the "Teen Opportunity" value prop.

## 💰 In-App Promotions

14. **First Purchase Discount** — New users get their first order discounted or free delivery.
15. **"Harvest of the Week" Feature** — Spotlight a seller or a seasonal item on the home screen to drive engagement.
16. **Loyalty Tiers** — Bronze/Silver/Gold based on purchases. Gold members get exclusive early access to new listings.
17. **Seasonal Gift Cards** — "Give the gift of fresh, local produce" — great for holidays and birthdays.

## 📰 PR & Media

18. **Local News Pitches** — "App helps neighbors reduce food waste and share backyard harvests" is a feel-good story that local TV and newspapers love.
19. **Sustainability Angle** — Pitch to eco/sustainability blogs. The "Stop Food Waste" and "Beat Inflation" messaging is very timely.
20. **Podcast Sponsorships** — Sponsor local or gardening/sustainability podcasts.
